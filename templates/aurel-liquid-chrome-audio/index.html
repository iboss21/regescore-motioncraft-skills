03 · The craft

From molten pour to mirror finish.

  1. i

    The pour

    Steel is cast at 2,800 degrees Fahrenheit into a ceramic shell. One pour, one cup, no seams to hide.

  2. ii

    The grind

    Fourteen abrasives, each finer than the last, remove every trace of the one before it.

  3. iii

    The tune

    Every pair is measured against a reference curve and trimmed by a listener, not a script.

  4. iv

    The reveal

    Final buffing with cerium oxide until the cup reflects the room back, soft and exact.
